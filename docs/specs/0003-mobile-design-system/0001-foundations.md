# Foundations

## Summary

The foundation uses neutral surfaces, refined emerald actions, one sans reading family, moderate curves, comfortable spacing, and complete light and dark themes. Lime remains a small brand spark and never becomes a large content surface.

## Typography

* `display` is Instrument Sans semibold, 40 by 48.
* `figure` is Instrument Sans semibold, 30 by 38.
* `title` is Instrument Sans bold, 28 by 34.
* `heading` is Instrument Sans semibold, 20 by 26.
* `subheading` is Instrument Sans semibold, 16 by 22.
* `body` is Instrument Sans regular, 16 by 24.
* `bodySmall` is Instrument Sans regular, 14 by 20.
* `action` is Instrument Sans semibold, 16 by 20.
* `label` is Instrument Sans medium, 14 by 20.
* `eyebrow` is Instrument Sans semibold, 12 by 16.
* `caption` is Instrument Sans regular, 12 by 16.
* `amount` and `amountSmall` use Instrument Sans medium with tabular digits.
* `code`, `ticker`, OTP, PIN, wallet addresses, and transaction references use Geist Mono.

Financial figures may step down from `display` to `figure` when width is constrained. They may use a controlled second line only after the smaller token cannot fit.

## Color

Light theme uses canvas `#F7F8FA`, surface `#FFFFFF`, raised surface `#F1F3F5`, primary text `#101114`, secondary text `#5F6670`, border `#E4E7EB`, and emerald action `#0B7A53`.

Dark theme uses canvas `#0B0D10`, surface `#12151A`, raised surface `#191D23`, primary text `#F4F6F8`, secondary text `#A2A9B3`, border `#252A32`, and emerald action `#45D6A0`.

Status colors remain separate from the action family. Lime is decorative only and cannot carry body text or financial status.

## Spacing and geometry

The spacing grid uses 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 40, 48, and 56 points. Screen gutter is 20. Card padding is 16. Section gap is 28. Every interactive target is at least 48 points.

Controls use a 12 point curve. Cards use 16. Sheets use 24. Full circles are reserved for icon wells, badges, and navigation indicators.

## Theme

Theme preference is `system`, `light`, or `dark`. System is the default. A saved light or dark choice overrides the device until the user chooses system again.

## Motion

Motion is expressive in onboarding, successful transactions, live values, and navigation. Forms and reading surfaces remain calm. Reduced motion removes continuous movement, translation, scaling, and shaking. Short opacity feedback may remain.

## Accessibility

Text reflows at larger device settings. Controls do not clip labels. Text contrast reaches 4.5 to 1. Meaningful controls reach 3 to 1. Status never relies on color alone. Every icon action has a spoken label and a 48 point target.
