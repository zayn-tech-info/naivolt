# Verification

## Required checks

* Render light and dark themes at compact, standard, and large phone widths.
* Render normal text and the largest supported device text setting.
* Check short, long, hidden, loading, empty, and failed balance states.
* Check that every interactive target is at least 48 points.
* Check that every icon action has a spoken label and state.
* Check text contrast at 4.5 to 1 and meaningful controls at 3 to 1.
* Check expressive motion and reduced motion on Android and iOS.
* Check Sell, Deposit, Withdraw, headers, and bottom navigation routes.
* Run TypeScript, ESLint, and the design audit.

## Home evidence

Capture the home screen in light and dark themes at compact width. Capture one large text state and one long balance state. Confirm that the three quick actions align and that no Buy action appears.
